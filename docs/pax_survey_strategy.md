# Pax Survey Strategy: "Circular Savings in the Digital Age"

## Overview
This survey is designed to capture market research data from all participants on the Pax platform, regardless of whether they currently participate in a Chama. It acts as an educational funnel, introducing the concept of mobile-first circular savings, gathering data on current saving habits, and capturing emails for the ChamaPay waitlist.

## Survey Structure

### Section 1: Demographics & Awareness
1. **What is your age bracket?**
   * [ ] 18-24
   * [ ] 25-34
   * [ ] 35-44
   * [ ] 45+
2. **Are you familiar with the concept of a "Chama" or "Merry-Go-Round" (a group of people pooling money together and rotating the payout)?**
   * [ ] Yes, I participate in one currently.
   * [ ] Yes, I know of it or know people who do it, but I don't participate.
   * [ ] No, I have never heard of this.

*If they answered "No", provide a brief explainer text:*
> *"A Merry-Go-Round (or Chama) is a traditional way of saving where a group of friends or colleagues contribute a fixed amount of money weekly or monthly. The total collection is given to one member each time, rotating until everyone has received a payout. It builds financial discipline and trust!"*

### Section 2: Current Financial Habits
3. **How do you currently save the majority of your money?**
   * [ ] Mobile Money (M-Shwari, KCB M-Pesa, etc.)
   * [ ] Traditional Bank Account
   * [ ] Crypto / Stablecoins (USDC, USDT)
   * [ ] Cash (At home)
   * [ ] Sacco / Investment Groups
4. **What is the biggest challenge you face when trying to save money consistently?**
   * [ ] Lack of discipline / spending it too easily.
   * [ ] Inflation / money losing value.
   * [ ] Not earning enough to save.
   * [ ] I don't trust banks or institutions.

### Section 3: The "What If" (Concept Testing)
5. **If there was a mobile app that allowed you to form a secure "Merry-Go-Round" with your trusted friends—automating the payouts directly to M-Pesa so you never have to chase anyone for money—would you try it?**
   * [ ] Yes, absolutely. It sounds convenient.
   * [ ] Maybe, but I would need to trust the app first.
   * [ ] No, I prefer saving alone.

### Section 4: Chama Specifics (Logic Branch: Only if they answered "Yes, I participate" in Q2)
6. **How would you categorize the group you currently save with?**
   * [ ] Family members
   * [ ] Workplace colleagues
   * [ ] Friends / Social circle
   * [ ] Business / Investment partners
7. **What is the most frustrating part of your current Chama experience?**
   * [ ] Members paying late.
   * [ ] Lack of transparency (not knowing exactly how much is in the pot).
   * [ ] Relying on one person (the treasurer) to hold and manage the money.

### Section 5: The Pitch & Waitlist
*Text Block:*
> *"We are building **ChamaPay**, an app that brings circular savings into the digital age. It uses automated smart contracts to manage contributions via M-Pesa, ensuring total transparency and guaranteed payouts. No more missing funds, no more chasing members."*

   * *[ Open Text Field ]*

## Budget & Targeting Strategy
*   **Target Sample Size:** 300 participants in Kenya.
*   **Payout per Survey:** $0.30 USDC.
*   **Total Expected Cost:** $90 USDC (approx. 11,700 KES).
*   **Total Budget Allocated:** ~13,000 KES.
*   **Rationale:** 300 participants provides a statistically significant sample size to validate assumptions without overspending. At $0.30 USDC (approx. 40 KES for a 2-3 minute survey), the incentive is highly competitive for the Kenyan micro-task market and should result in rapid survey completion.
